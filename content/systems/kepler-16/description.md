# Kepler-16 and its circumbinary planet

Kepler-16 is a stellar system about **245 light-years** away. The system contains two stars, with one host star carrying a mass of about **0.63 solar masses**, a radius of about **0.769 solar radii**, and a temperature of about **4020 K**. That star is smaller, cooler, and less massive than the Sun.

One known planet, **Kepler-16 b**, orbits in this system. Discovered in 2011 via transit, its radius is about **8.45 Earth radii**, larger than Earth's, and its mass is about **106 Earth masses**, also larger than Earth's. The planet's orbital period spans about **229 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year, with a mean density of about **0.967 g/cm³**, roughly **0.175 times Earth's** density.
