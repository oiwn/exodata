# LHS 1140: a cool red dwarf with two transiting planets

**LHS 1140** is a small, cool red dwarf star of spectral type **M4.5 V**, located about **48.9 light-years** away. The star's mass is about **0.184 solar masses**, its radius about **0.216 solar radii**, and its temperature about **3100 K**. These values make the star smaller, less massive, and cooler than the Sun. The star carries a reported lower limit of **5 billion years** for its age.

Two transiting planets orbit **LHS 1140**. Transit detection relies on a planet passing in front of its star, briefly dimming the starlight. **LHS 1140 b**, discovered in 2017, is a Super-Earth with a radius of about **1.73 Earth radii** and a mass of about **5.6 Earth masses**, both larger than Earth's. Its mean density is about **5.96 g/cm³**, roughly **1.08 times Earth's**. This planet's year lasts about **24.7 days**, far shorter than Earth's roughly **365-day year**.

**LHS 1140 c**, discovered in 2018, falls into the Earth-like category, which encompasses planets within about **1.5 times Earth's radius**. Its radius of about **1.27 Earth radii** and mass of about **1.91 Earth masses** both exceed Earth's. The planet's mean density is about **5.11 g/cm³**, about **0.928 times Earth's**. Its year spans about **3.78 days**, again much shorter than Earth's.

Of the two, **LHS 1140 c** has the shorter year at about **3.78 days**, while **LHS 1140 b** takes about **24.7 days**. By radius, **LHS 1140 c** is the smaller at about **1.27 Earth radii**, and **LHS 1140 b** the larger at about **1.73 Earth radii**. By mass, **LHS 1140 c** is the lighter at about **1.91 Earth masses**, with **LHS 1140 b** the heavier at about **5.6 Earth masses**.
