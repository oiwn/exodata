# HD 189733 and its planet

HD 189733 is a Sun-like star about 64.5 light-years away. The star's reported mass is about 0.85 solar masses, its radius about 0.68 solar radii, and its temperature about 5200 K — making it smaller, less massive, and cooler than the Sun. Its reported age has an upper limit of 1 billion years.

The system hosts one known planet, HD 189733 b, discovered in 2005 via radial velocity. HD 189733 b has a reported mass of about 359 Earth masses — roughly 1.13 times Jupiter's mass — and a reported radius of about 12.7 Earth radii, both larger than Earth's corresponding values. The planet completes its year in about 2.22 days, far shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.
