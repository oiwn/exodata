# Proxima Cen and its planets

**Proxima Cen** is an **M5.5 V** star, an M-type dwarf, which makes it a small, cool red dwarf. Its mass is **about 0.12 solar masses**, smaller than the Sun's, and its radius is **about 0.141 solar radii**, also smaller than the Sun's. The star's temperature is **about 3050 K**, cooler than the Sun's. The system lies **about 4.24 light-years** away.

Two planets are associated with **Proxima Cen**. Both were found with the radial velocity method, which measures the wobble a planet's gravity induces in its star's motion, since repeated wobbles reveal the planet and its orbital period. **Proxima Cen b** was discovered in 2016, and **Proxima Cen d** in 2025. Each minimum-mass quantity is a lower bound on the planet's true mass, since the orbit's tilt is unknown.

**Proxima Cen b** has a minimum-mass quantity of **about 1.06 Earth masses**, the largest minimum-mass quantity among the listed planets. Its year, or orbital period, is **about 11.2 days**, the longest year among the listed planets. That is shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**Proxima Cen d** has a minimum-mass quantity of **about 0.26 Earth masses**, which is smaller than Earth's, and the smallest minimum-mass quantity among the listed planets. Its year is **about 5.12 days**, the shortest among the listed planets, and also shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.
