# GJ 1214 and its planet

GJ 1214 is a small, cool red dwarf star (spectral type M4 V) located about 47.8 light-years away. The star's reported mass is about 0.178 solar masses, its radius is about 0.215 solar radii, and its temperature is about 3250 K — all estimates smaller and cooler than the Sun's corresponding values.

GJ 1214 hosts one known planet, GJ 1214 b, discovered in 2009 via the transit method, in which a planet passes in front of its star from our viewpoint, causing a small dip in observed brightness. The planet has a year of about 1.58 days, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year. GJ 1214 b's reported mass estimate is about 8.41 Earth masses, larger than Earth's, and its reported radius estimate is about 2.73 Earth radii, also larger than Earth's.
