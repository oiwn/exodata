# GJ 1214 and Its Neptune-like Planet

**GJ 1214** is a small, cool red dwarf of spectral type **M4 V**, lying about **47.8 light-years** from us. Its mass is **about 0.178 solar masses** and its radius is **about 0.215 solar radii**, both smaller than the Sun's corresponding values. At **about 3250 K**, the star is cooler than the Sun.

One planet is associated with this host. **GJ 1214 b** was found by the transit method in 2009, when it passed in front of its star and produced a small dip in observed brightness; repeated dips helped establish its orbital period. That year is **about 1.58 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

The planet is classified as Neptune-like. Its radius is **about 2.73 Earth radii**, larger than Earth's, and its mass, which is not a minimum-mass quantity, is **about 8.41 Earth masses**, also larger than Earth's. From these, its mean density is **about 2.27 g/cm³**, **about 0.412 times Earth's**.
