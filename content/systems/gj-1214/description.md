# GJ 1214: a single planet around a small red dwarf

**GJ 1214** is a star with a spectral type of **M4 V**, classifying it as a small, cool red dwarf. Its mass is about **0.178 solar masses**, smaller than the Sun's, and its radius is about **0.215 solar radii**, also smaller than the Sun's. The star is cooler than the Sun, with a temperature of about **3250 K**. This system lies at a distance of about **47.8 light-years**.

One planet, **GJ 1214 b**, is associated with this host. Discovered in 2009 via the transit method, the planet passes in front of its star, causing repeated dips in brightness that help establish its orbital period. **GJ 1214 b** completes its year in about **1.58 days**, which is shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Its radius is about **2.73 Earth radii**, larger than Earth's, placing it in the Neptune-like size class. The planet's mass is about **8.41 Earth masses**, also larger than Earth's. From these values, **GJ 1214 b** has a mean density of about **2.27 g/cm³**, roughly **0.412 times Earth's** density.
