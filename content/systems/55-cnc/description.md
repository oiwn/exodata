# 55 Cnc: a Sun-like star with five known planets

**55 Cnc** is a G-type dwarf star about **41 light-years** away, with an age of about **9.5 billion years**. Its mass of about **0.96 solar masses** is smaller than the Sun's, its radius of about **0.93 solar radii** is smaller than the Sun's, and its temperature of about **5240 K** is cooler than the Sun's. Five planets are associated with this host.

**55 Cnc e**, a Super-Earth, has the shortest year among the listed planets at about **0.737 days**. Its mass is about **7.99 Earth masses**, its radius about **1.88 Earth radii**, and its mean density about **6.68 g/cm³**, roughly **1.21 times Earth's**. Both its radius and mass are larger than Earth's, while its year is shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**55 Cnc d** has the longest year among the listed planets at about **4800 days**, longer than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Its minimum-mass quantity is about **1210 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's and roughly **3.82 times Jupiter's mass**.

**55 Cnc b**, discovered in 1996, has the second-shortest year among the listed planets at about **14.7 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Its minimum-mass quantity is about **276 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's. **55 Cnc c** has a minimum-mass quantity of about **56.6 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's, with a year of about **44.4 days**, also shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**55 Cnc f** has the second-longest year among the listed planets at about **261 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Its minimum-mass quantity is about **48.5 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's. The radial velocity method, which measures the wobble a planet's gravity induces in its star's motion, revealed these planets between 1996 and 2007.
