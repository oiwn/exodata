# 55 Cnc and its five planets

**55 Cnc** is a **G8V** main-sequence star located about **41 light-years** away. Its mass is about **0.96 solar masses**, smaller than the Sun's, and its radius is about **0.93 solar radii**, also smaller than the Sun's. With a temperature of about **5240 K**, it is cooler than the Sun. The star's age is about **9.5 billion years**. Five planets are described here.

**55 Cnc b** was discovered in 1996 using the radial velocity method, which measures the wobble a planet's gravity induces in its star's motion. Its minimum-mass quantity is about **276 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's, and its year is about **14.7 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Among the listed planets, **55 Cnc b** has the second-shortest year at about **14.7 days**.

**55 Cnc c** was found in 2004, also via radial velocity. Its minimum-mass quantity is about **56.6 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's, and its year of about **44.4 days** is shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**55 Cnc d**, discovered in 2002, has the longest year among the listed planets at about **4800 days**, longer than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Its minimum-mass quantity is about **1210 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's and roughly **3.82 times Jupiter's mass**.

**55 Cnc e**, a Super-Earth discovered in 2004, has the shortest year among the listed planets at about **0.737 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Its mass is about **7.99 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's, and its radius is about **1.88 Earth radii**, larger than Earth's. Its mean density is about **6.68 g/cm³**, roughly **1.21 times Earth's**.

**55 Cnc f**, discovered in 2007, has a minimum-mass quantity of about **48.5 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's. Its year is about **261 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year, and it has the second-longest year among the listed planets, at about **261 days**.
