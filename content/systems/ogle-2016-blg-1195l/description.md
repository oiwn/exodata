# OGLE-2016-BLG-1195L and Its Planet

**OGLE-2016-BLG-1195L** is a star with a mass of **about 0.37 solar masses**, smaller than the Sun's mass. It lies at a distance of **about 23500 light-years** from us.

One planet is associated with this host, **OGLE-2016-BLG-1195L b**, found in 2017 by microlensing. Its mass is **about 1.43 Earth masses**, larger than Earth's. This is a planet mass rather than a minimum-mass quantity. No orbital period is reported for **OGLE-2016-BLG-1195L b**.
