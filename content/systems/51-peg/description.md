# 51 Peg and its planet

51 Peg is a Sun-like G-type dwarf star, estimated to be about 6.76 billion years old. The star's reported mass estimate is larger than the Sun's, at about 1.05 solar masses, and its reported radius estimate is also larger than the Sun's, at about 1.15 solar radii. Its estimated temperature of about 5790 K makes it hotter than the Sun. The system lies roughly 50.4 light-years away.

The star hosts one known planet, 51 Peg b, discovered in 1995. This planet has a reported mass estimate of about 194 Earth masses, which is larger than Earth's mass. Its year is remarkably short at about 4.23 days, far shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.
