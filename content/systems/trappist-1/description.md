# TRAPPIST-1: seven Earth-sized worlds around a cool red dwarf

**TRAPPIST-1** is a small, cool red dwarf star with a spectral type of **M8.0 V**. Its mass of about **0.0898 solar masses** and radius of about **0.119 solar radii** are both smaller than the Sun's, and its temperature of about **2570 K** makes it cooler than the Sun as well. The star is about **7.6 billion years** old. Around this star orbit seven planets, each identified through the transit method, where a planet passes in front of its star and causes a dip in brightness.

**TRAPPIST-1 b** is Earth-like, with a radius of about **1.12 Earth radii** and a mass of about **1.37 Earth masses**, both larger than Earth's. Its mean density of about **5.45 g/cm³**, about **0.989 times Earth's**, and its year lasts about **1.51 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Among the listed planets, it has the shortest year and the second-largest radius, and it carries the largest mass.

**TRAPPIST-1 c** is also Earth-like, with a radius of about **1.1 Earth radii** and a mass of about **1.31 Earth masses**, both larger than Earth's. Its mean density is about **5.46 g/cm³**, roughly **0.991 times Earth's**, and its year of about **2.42 days** is shorter than Earth's. It holds the second-shortest year among the group.

**TRAPPIST-1 d** is a Sub-Earth, meaning its radius of about **0.788 Earth radii** is smaller than Earth's, and its mass of about **0.388 Earth masses** is likewise smaller. Its mean density of about **4.37 g/cm³** is about **0.793 times Earth's**, and its year of about **4.05 days** is shorter than Earth's. It has the second-smallest radius and second-smallest mass among the listed planets.

**TRAPPIST-1 e** is also a Sub-Earth, with a radius of about **0.92 Earth radii** and a mass of about **0.692 Earth masses**, both smaller than Earth's. Its mean density is about **4.9 g/cm³**, about **0.889 times Earth's**, and its year lasts about **6.1 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**TRAPPIST-1 f** is Earth-like, with a radius of about **1.05 Earth radii** and a mass of about **1.04 Earth masses**, both larger than Earth's. Its mean density is about **5.02 g/cm³**, about **0.91 times Earth's**, and its year of about **9.21 days** is shorter than Earth's.

**TRAPPIST-1 g** is Earth-like as well, with a radius of about **1.13 Earth radii** and a mass of about **1.32 Earth masses**, both larger than Earth's. Its mean density is about **5.06 g/cm³**, about **0.918 times Earth's**, and its year of about **12.4 days** is shorter than Earth's. Among the listed planets, it has the largest radius, the second-longest year, and the second-largest mass.

**TRAPPIST-1 h** is a Sub-Earth with the smallest radius of about **0.755 Earth radii** and the smallest mass of about **0.326 Earth masses**, both smaller than Earth's. Its mean density is about **4.17 g/cm³**, about **0.757 times Earth's**, and its year of about **18.8 days**, while shorter than Earth's, is the longest among the listed planets.
