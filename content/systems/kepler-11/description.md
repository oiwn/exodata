# Kepler-11: six transiting planets around a Sun-like star

**Kepler-11** is a star with a mass of **about 0.95 solar masses**, a radius of **about 1.1 solar radii**, and a temperature of **about 5680 K**. Its age is **about 8 billion years**. Six planets orbit the star, all discovered in 2010 through the transit method, in which a planet passing in front of its star causes a small dip in observed brightness.

**Kepler-11 b** is a Super-Earth with a radius larger than Earth's at **about 1.8 Earth radii** and a mass of **about 1.9 Earth masses**. Its mean density is **about 1.8 g/cm³**, or **about 0.326 times Earth's**. The planet completes its year in **about 10.3 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year. Among the listed planets, **Kepler-11 b** has the shortest year and the smallest radius.

**Kepler-11 c** is a Neptune-like planet with a radius larger than Earth's at **about 2.87 Earth radii** and a mass larger than Earth's at **about 2.9 Earth masses**. Its mean density is **about 0.676 g/cm³**, or **about 0.123 times Earth's**. **Kepler-11 c** has a year of **about 13 days**, the second-shortest among the listed planets and shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**Kepler-11 d** is a Neptune-like planet with a radius larger than Earth's at **about 3.12 Earth radii** and a mass larger than Earth's at **about 7.3 Earth masses**. Its mean density is **about 1.32 g/cm³**, or **about 0.24 times Earth's**. **Kepler-11 d** completes its orbit in **about 22.7 days**, a year shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**Kepler-11 e** is a Jupiter-like planet with the largest radius among the listed planets at **about 4.19 Earth radii**, larger than Earth's. Its mass is larger than Earth's at **about 8 Earth masses**, and its mean density is **about 0.599 g/cm³**, or **about 0.109 times Earth's**. **Kepler-11 e** has a year of **about 32 days**, shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**Kepler-11 f** is a Super-Earth with a radius larger than Earth's at **about 2.49 Earth radii**, the second-smallest among the listed planets. Its mass is larger than Earth's at **about 2 Earth masses**, and its mean density is **about 0.714 g/cm³**, or **about 0.13 times Earth's**. **Kepler-11 f** has the second-longest year among the listed planets at **about 46.7 days**, still shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.

**Kepler-11 g** is a Neptune-like planet with a radius larger than Earth's at **about 3.33 Earth radii**, the second-largest among the listed planets. Its mass is an upper limit of **25 Earth masses**. **Kepler-11 g** has the longest year among the listed planets at **about 118 days**, which remains shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year.
