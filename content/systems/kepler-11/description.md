# Kepler-11 and its six planets

**Kepler-11** is a star located about **2110 light-years** away. It has a mass of about **0.95 solar masses**, a radius of about **1.1 solar radii**, and a temperature of about **5680 K**. Its age is about **8 billion years**.

Six planets are associated with **Kepler-11**. All were discovered in 2010 using the transit method, which detects the small dip in a star's brightness when a planet passes in front of it from our viewpoint; repeated dips help establish orbital periods. The planets are **Kepler-11 b**, **Kepler-11 c**, **Kepler-11 d**, **Kepler-11 e**, **Kepler-11 f**, and **Kepler-11 g**.

**Kepler-11 b** is a Super-Earth with a radius of about **1.8 Earth radii**. Its mass is about **1.9 Earth masses**, and its mean density is about **1.8 g/cm³**, about **0.326 times Earth's**. It completes an orbit in about **10.3 days**.

**Kepler-11 c** is Neptune-like, with a radius of about **2.87 Earth radii** and a mass of about **2.9 Earth masses**. Its mean density is about **0.676 g/cm³**, about **0.123 times Earth's**. Its orbital period is about **13 days**.

**Kepler-11 d** is also Neptune-like, with a radius of about **3.12 Earth radii** and a mass of about **7.3 Earth masses**. Its mean density is about **1.32 g/cm³**, about **0.24 times Earth's**. It orbits in about **22.7 days**.

**Kepler-11 e** is Jupiter-like, with the largest radius among the listed planets at about **4.19 Earth radii**. Its mass is about **8 Earth masses**, and its mean density is about **0.599 g/cm³**, about **0.109 times Earth's**. Its orbital period is about **32 days**.

**Kepler-11 f** is a Super-Earth with a radius of about **2.49 Earth radii** and a mass of about **2 Earth masses**. Its mean density is about **0.714 g/cm³**, about **0.13 times Earth's**. It completes an orbit in about **46.7 days**.

**Kepler-11 g** is Neptune-like, with a radius of about **3.33 Earth radii**. Its mass is an upper limit of **25 Earth masses**. Its orbital period is about **118 days**.

Among the listed planets, **Kepler-11 b** has the shortest year at about **10.3 days**, and the smallest radius at about **1.8 Earth radii**. **Kepler-11 g** has the longest year at about **118 days** and the second-largest radius at about **3.33 Earth radii**. **Kepler-11 c** has the second-shortest year at about **13 days**. **Kepler-11 f** has the second-longest year at about **46.7 days** and the second-smallest radius at about **2.49 Earth radii**. **Kepler-11 e** has the largest radius at about **4.19 Earth radii**. The year of each of these planets is shorter than Earth's roughly 365-day year, and the radius of each is larger than Earth's. The masses of **Kepler-11 c**, **Kepler-11 d**, **Kepler-11 e**, and **Kepler-11 f** are larger than Earth's.
